UNDERSTAND YOUR BODY

The Four Phases of Your Cycle

Learn about the four phases of the menstrual cycle.

01

Menstruation

The uterine lining sheds, resulting in menstrual bleeding. This marks the beginning of the menstrual cycle.

02

Follicular Phase

Hormonal changes support the development of ovarian follicles, and the uterine lining begins to build up again.

03

Ovulation

An ovary releases an egg. The timing varies, and a calendar estimate cannot confirm when ovulation actually occurs.

04

Luteal Phase

After ovulation, hormonal changes prepare the uterine lining. If pregnancy does not occur, another period usually begins.

ABOUT LUNACYCLE

A Little More Awareness, Every Month

LunaCycle is a menstrual cycle education project designed to make cycle tracking easier to understand.

Our goal is to help users learn about their menstrual cycle and explore estimated period, ovulation, and fertile-window dates in one simple place.

LunaCycle provides educational estimates and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
